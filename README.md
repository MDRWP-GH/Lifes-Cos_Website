Lifes'Cos Wedsite is a platform dedicated to sharing knowledge and resources about Cosplay Photo Album.

Developed By the Lifes'Cos

We aim to create a vibrant community where enthusiasts can connect, share their experiences, and showcase their creativity through cosplay photography.

Join us on this exciting journey as we celebrate the art of cosplay and the passion behind it. Whether you're a seasoned cosplayer or just starting out, Lifes'Cos is here to support you every step of the way.

Frameworks and technologies used in our website include Next.js, Nest,js, Node.js, MongoDB and Express.

Language used: JavaScript, TypeScript, HTML, CSS, Tailwindcss and SQL
